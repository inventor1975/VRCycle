\section{Position}
\label{sec:setsztl_position}

This chapter opens \textbf{VR Part II}: raising VR onto ZTL --- Zero-Trust
Logic, the curator's three-symbol logic of ``truth is never granted on
credit'' (Zenodo, DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.21318982}; the measured Python
twin of this chapter is \texttt{zopsets.py}, expedition E21, in the ZTL
repository).  Step~(а) of the programme: the \emph{witnessed identity} of the
operational set universe (Chapter~\ref{chap:setsop}) \emph{is} a ZTL atom
discipline.  An identity atom $x \approx y$ earns \textsf{T} by carrying a
bisimulation, earns \textsf{F} by carrying a refutation (apartness at a finite
separation stage), and is worth the input mark \textsf{Z} until examined.
Nothing is translated between the two sides --- the disciplines coincide;
the Lean development makes the coincidence a set of kernel-checked theorems.

The ZTL value kernel is \emph{vendored} into
\texttt{VRCycle/SetsZTL/Kernel.lean} with a commit pin (the dependency is
one-way: ZTL never depends on VR).  The kernel stands on the \emph{empty}
axiom list, as it does at home; the whole verdict layer of this chapter is
likewise \axiomprofile{[]}, with \axiomprofile{[propext]} entering only
through \texttt{vn\_inj} of Chapter~\ref{chap:setsop}.

\begin{definition}[The verdict carried with its certificate]
\label{def:setsztl_eqverdict}
\lean{VRCycle.SetsZTL.EqVerdict, VRCycle.SetsZTL.EqVerdict.val}
\leanok
\uses{def:setsop_opset}
\texttt{EqVerdict x y} has three constructors: \texttt{earnedT} holding a
bisimulation witness $x \approx y$, \texttt{earnedF} holding a refutation,
and \texttt{unexamined}; \texttt{val} sends them to \textsf{T}, \textsf{F},
\textsf{Z}.  Soundness is not a theorem about the type --- it is its shape:
\texttt{sound\_T}/\texttt{sound\_F} extract the certificate from the value
(\axiomprofile{[]}).  The zero-trust start is \texttt{unexamined}
(\texttt{zero\_trust\_start}: its value is \textsf{Z}).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The alive rules are witness constructors]
\label{thm:setsztl_constructors}
\lean{VRCycle.SetsZTL.EqVerdict.refl_val, VRCycle.SetsZTL.EqVerdict.symm_val, VRCycle.SetsZTL.EqVerdict.composeT_val}
\leanok
\uses{def:setsztl_eqverdict, thm:setsop_equiv}
\texttt{refl} (the diagonal), \texttt{symm} (the converse, verdict-preserving)
and \texttt{composeT} (composition of carried witnesses via
\texttt{Equiv.trans}: two earned identities earn the third) --- each
\axiomprofile{[]}.  The ZTL inference rules alive on $\approx$-atoms are not
table luck; each is backed by a certificate constructor.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The vn register is total and classical]
\label{thm:setsztl_vn}
\lean{VRCycle.SetsZTL.vnVerdict_ne_Z, VRCycle.SetsZTL.vnVerdict_T_iff}
\leanok
\uses{def:setsztl_eqverdict}
On the operational naturals every identity atom decides:
\texttt{vnVerdict n m} is never \textsf{Z}, and its value is \textsf{T}
exactly when $n = m$ (\textsf{F} earned through \texttt{vn\_inj}).  Identity
on this register is \emph{totally earnable} --- the Lean twin of E21~\S1.
Both \axiomprofile{[propext]}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Groundedness is orthogonal to earnability]
\label{thm:setsztl_orthogonality}
\lean{VRCycle.SetsZTL.quine_orthogonality, VRCycle.SetsZTL.quineVsEmpty_val}
\leanok
\uses{def:setsztl_eqverdict}
The Quine atom $\Omega = \{\Omega\}$ earns its identity
(\texttt{refl} gives \textsf{T}) while $\neg\mathrm{IsGrounded}\,\Omega$;
and $\Omega$ earns \textsf{F} against $\varnothing$ --- apartness across the
grounded/ungrounded divide.  The cycle that dooms a sentence (the liar:
permanent quarantine, an lfp leftover) is harmless in a set (identity is
gfp-shaped): AFA-as-theorem is the set-side mirror of
quarantine-as-theorem.  \axiomprofile{[]} and \axiomprofile{[propext]}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The fully-earned register is classical]
\label{thm:setsztl_classical}
\lean{VRCycle.SetsZTL.earned_register_classical, VRCycle.SetsZTL.earned_register_ne_Z, VRCycle.SetsZTL.earned_register_T_iff}
\leanok
\uses{def:setsztl_eqverdict}
If every atom of a formula carries a decided, truthful verdict, then the
greedy ZTL value of \emph{every} formula over the six connectives is decided
and truthful: \textsf{T} exactly on the classically true formulas, \textsf{Z}
nowhere.  Over verified identity atoms the zero-trust connectives neither lie
nor refuse.  Proved by one structural induction with a per-connective
\texttt{Decided} calculus; \axiomprofile{[]} (empty axiom list --- no
\texttt{propext}).
\end{theorem}
