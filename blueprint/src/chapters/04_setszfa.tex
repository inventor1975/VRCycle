\section{Position}
\label{sec:setszfa_position}

VR-Sets-ZFA extends VR-Sets with a coinductive universe that admits
non-well-founded sets and proves Aczel's Anti-Foundation Axiom (AFA) as a
theorem.  The chapter constructively resolves Conjecture~IV.2 from
Chapter~\ref{chap:sets}: the required type $U$ satisfying AFA with
extensional membership exists and is machine-verified.

\textbf{Architectural fact.} The construction is built on mathlib's
\texttt{PFunctor.M} (Avigad--Carneiro--Hudon 2019), the greatest fixpoint of
the pre-set functor.  \texttt{CoPSet} is the coinductive counterpart of
mathlib's inductive \texttt{PSet}: both use branching type $(\alpha :
\mathrm{Type}\,u,\, A : \alpha \to \cdot)$; the difference is the
generation principle (greatest vs.\ least fixpoint).  \texttt{OSetZFA} is
the quotient of \texttt{CoPSet} by extensional cobisimulation, the
coinductive analogue of \texttt{OSet = ZFSet = Quotient PSet.setoid}.

AFA follows from the universal property of the final coalgebra: every
directed graph has a unique decoration, proved by \texttt{CoPSet.corec}
(existence) and \texttt{CoPSet.bisim\_imp\_Equiv} (uniqueness).  No
coinductive axiom is added; the result lies entirely within
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.

\section{Architecture (Part II)}
\label{sec:setszfa_arch}

\begin{definition}[Pre-set functor and coinductive pre-sets]
\label{def:CoPSetFunctor}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSetFunctor, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet}
\leanok
$\texttt{CoPSetFunctor} : \texttt{PFunctor}$ with shape type
$\texttt{A} := \mathrm{Type}\,u$ and branching $\texttt{B} := \mathrm{id}$.
$\texttt{CoPSet}.{u} := \texttt{PFunctor.M CoPSetFunctor}.{u}$
(greatest fixpoint of \texttt{CoPSetFunctor}).
Elements of \texttt{CoPSet} are potentially infinite or cyclic trees;
in contrast, elements of \texttt{PSet} (inductive, mathlib) are
always well-founded.  Universe: \texttt{CoPSet}.{u} : \texttt{Type
(u+1)}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[CoPSet destructor, constructor, and projections]
\label{def:CoPSet_ops}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.dest, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.shape,
      VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.children, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.mk,
      VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.dest_mk, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.mk_dest}
\leanok
\uses{def:CoPSetFunctor}
$\texttt{CoPSet.dest}(x) := \texttt{PFunctor.M.dest}(x) : \Sigma\,\alpha :
\mathrm{Type}\,u,\; \alpha \to \texttt{CoPSet}$.
Shape $:= (-).\texttt{dest}.1$; children $:= (-).\texttt{dest}.2$.
Constructor: $\texttt{CoPSet.mk}(\alpha, A) :=
\texttt{PFunctor.M.mk}\,\langle\alpha, A\rangle$.
Roundtrip: \texttt{dest\_mk} and \texttt{mk\_dest} hold by \texttt{rfl}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Corecursor]
\label{def:CoPSet_corec}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.corec, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.dest_corec}
\leanok
\uses{def:CoPSetFunctor}
For any type $X$ and coalgebra $f : X \to \texttt{CoPSetFunctor}\,X$,
$\texttt{CoPSet.corec}(f) : X \to \texttt{CoPSet}$ is the unique
coalgebra morphism into the final coalgebra.
Computation rule: $\texttt{dest}(\texttt{corec}(f)(x)) =
f(x)[\texttt{corec}(f)]$ --- proved by \texttt{rfl} (definitional, not
axiomatic).
This is the \emph{existence} half of AFA.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Cobisimulation and extensional equivalence]
\label{def:CoPSet_equiv}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.isBisim, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.Equiv,
      VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.Equiv.refl, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.Equiv.symm,
      VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.Equiv.trans, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.isBisim_Equiv,
      VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.instSetoid, VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.bisim_imp_Equiv}
\leanok
\uses{def:CoPSet_ops}
$\texttt{CoPSet.isBisim}(R)$: a relation $R$ is a bisimulation when
for every $(c, d) \in R$, the shapes of $c$ and $d$ are in bijection
carrying $R$-related children to $R$-related children.
$\texttt{CoPSet.Equiv}(x, y) := \exists R,\; \texttt{isBisim}(R) \land R\,x\,y$
(greatest bisimulation; reflexive, symmetric, transitive).
Key: $\texttt{bisim\_imp\_Equiv}$ --- if $R$ is a bisimulation and $R\,x\,y$,
then $\texttt{Equiv}\,x\,y$.  This is the \emph{uniqueness} half of AFA.
\texttt{CoPSet.instSetoid} packages \texttt{Equiv} as a Lean setoid.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[$\mathrm{OSetZFA}$ --- the ZFA universe]
\label{def:OSetZFA}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.mk,
      VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.eq_iff, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.sound,
      VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.exact, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.mk_surjective}
\leanok
\uses{def:CoPSet_equiv}
$\texttt{OSetZFA} := \texttt{Quotient CoPSet.instSetoid}$ (cobisimulation
quotient of \texttt{CoPSet}).
$\texttt{OSetZFA.mk}(x) := \llbracket x \rrbracket$ (quotient constructor).
$\texttt{sound}$: $x \approx y \Rightarrow \texttt{mk}(x) = \texttt{mk}(y)$.
$\texttt{exact}$: $\texttt{mk}(x) = \texttt{mk}(y) \Rightarrow x \approx y$.
$\texttt{mk\_surjective}$: every element of \texttt{OSetZFA} has a
\texttt{CoPSet} representative.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Membership on $\mathrm{OSetZFA}$]
\label{def:OSetZFA_mem}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet.mem, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.Mem,
      VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.instMembership, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.mem_mk,
      VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.ext, VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA.ext_iff}
\leanok
\uses{def:OSetZFA, def:CoPSet_ops}
$\texttt{CoPSet.mem}(x, y) := \exists\, i : y.\texttt{shape},\;
x \approx y.\texttt{children}(i)$.
Lifted to $\texttt{OSetZFA.Mem}$ via \texttt{OSetZFA.lift}; well-definedness by
\texttt{CoPSet.mem\_congr}.
\texttt{mem\_mk}: $a \in \llbracket b \rrbracket \leftrightarrow
\exists\, i : b.\texttt{shape},\; a = \llbracket b.\texttt{children}(i) \rrbracket$.
Extensionality: $\llbracket x \rrbracket = \llbracket y \rrbracket \leftrightarrow
\forall z,\; z \in \llbracket x \rrbracket \leftrightarrow z \in \llbracket y \rrbracket$.
\end{definition}

\section{AFA as theorem (Part III)}
\label{sec:setszfa_afa}

\begin{definition}[Decoration predicate and graph coalgebra]
\label{def:isDecoration}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.isDecoration, VR.SetsZFA.graphCoalg}
\leanok
\uses{def:OSetZFA_mem}
$\texttt{isDecoration}(E, f) :=
\forall v : V,\; \forall x : \mathrm{OSetZFA},\;
x \in f(v) \leftrightarrow \exists\, w : V,\; E\,v\,w \land x = f(w)$.
$\texttt{graphCoalg}(E)(v) := \langle\{w \mid E\,v\,w\},
\texttt{Subtype.val}\rangle \in \texttt{CoPSetFunctor}\,V$
(the out-neighbourhood coalgebra for vertex $v$).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Graph CoPSet and decoration]
\label{def:graphDecoration}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.graphCoPSet, VR.SetsZFA.graphCoPSet_dest,
      VR.SetsZFA.graphDecoration}
\leanok
\uses{def:isDecoration, def:CoPSet_corec}
$\texttt{graphCoPSet}(E) := \texttt{CoPSet.corec}(\texttt{graphCoalg}(E))
: V \to \texttt{CoPSet}$
(the unique coalgebra morphism from $(V, \texttt{graphCoalg}(E))$ to
$(\texttt{CoPSet}, \texttt{CoPSet.dest})$).
Computation: $\texttt{dest}(\texttt{graphCoPSet}(E)(v)) =
\langle\{w \mid E\,v\,w\},\; w \mapsto \texttt{graphCoPSet}(E)(w)\rangle$
(\texttt{graphCoPSet\_dest} by \texttt{rfl}).
$\texttt{graphDecoration}(E)(v) := \llbracket\texttt{graphCoPSet}(E)(v)\rrbracket
: \mathrm{OSetZFA}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[AFA existence and uniqueness]
\label{thm:graphDecoration_exists}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.graphDecoration_isDecoration, VR.SetsZFA.graphDecoration_unique}
\leanok
\uses{def:graphDecoration, def:CoPSet_equiv}
\texttt{graphDecoration\_isDecoration}: $\texttt{graphDecoration}(E)$ is
a decoration of $(V, E)$.  Proof: unfold via \texttt{mem\_mk} and
\texttt{graphCoPSet\_dest}; the shape is definitionally $\{w \mid E\,v\,w\}$;
\texttt{OSetZFA.sound} and \texttt{OSetZFA.exact} close the iff.

\texttt{graphDecoration\_unique}: if $f$ is any decoration of $(V, E)$,
then $f = \texttt{graphDecoration}(E)$.  Proof by coinductive bisimulation
at \texttt{CoPSet}-level: choose \texttt{Classical.choice} representatives
$\texttt{fRep}(v)$ for each $f(v)$; define $R(c, d) := \exists\, v,\;
c \approx \texttt{fRep}(v) \land d \approx \texttt{graphCoPSet}(E)(v)$; show
$R$ is a bisimulation by tracing decoration conditions; apply
\texttt{CoPSet.bisim\_imp\_Equiv} to get $\texttt{fRep}(v) \approx
\texttt{graphCoPSet}(E)(v)$; conclude $f(v) = \texttt{graphDecoration}(E)(v)$.

Direct induction is impossible (graphs may be non-well-founded);
\texttt{PFunctor.M.corec\_unique} is not applicable (it requires exact
coalgebra equality, not cobisimulation).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[AFA in OSetZFA]
\label{thm:AFA_in_OSetZFA}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.AFA_in_OSetZFA}
\leanok
\uses{def:isDecoration, thm:graphDecoration_exists}
$\forall (V : \mathrm{Type})\; (E : V \to V \to \mathrm{Prop}),\;
\exists!\, f : V \to \mathrm{OSetZFA},\; \texttt{isDecoration}(E, f)$.

AFA is a theorem in \texttt{OSetZFA}, not an axiom.  It follows from:
existence $=$ \texttt{graphDecoration\_isDecoration};
uniqueness $=$ \texttt{graphDecoration\_unique};
assembled as \texttt{ExistsUnique.intro}.

\noindent\axiomprofile{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.
No new axioms beyond the standard mathlib ceiling.
\end{theorem}

\section{Embedding $\mathrm{OSet} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{OSetZFA}$ (Part IV)}

\begin{definition}[Embedding of pre-sets and sets]
\label{def:embedOSet}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet, VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet_dest,
      VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet_congr, VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet_faithful,
      VR.SetsZFA.embedOSet, VR.SetsZFA.embedOSet_mk}
\leanok
\uses{def:OSet, def:OSetZFA, def:CoPSet_equiv}
$\texttt{embedPSet} : \texttt{PSet} \to \texttt{CoPSet}$ by structural
recursion: $\texttt{embedPSet}(\texttt{mk}(\alpha, A)) :=
\texttt{CoPSet.mk}(\alpha,\; i \mapsto \texttt{embedPSet}(A(i)))$.
Computation: \texttt{embedPSet\_dest} by \texttt{rfl}.

Forward: $\texttt{embedPSet\_congr}$: $\texttt{PSet.Equiv}\,x\,y \Rightarrow
\texttt{CoPSet.Equiv}(\texttt{embedPSet}(x), \texttt{embedPSet}(y))$.
Proved by coinductive bisimulation (no induction on PSet needed).

Backward: $\texttt{embedPSet\_faithful}$: $\texttt{CoPSet.Equiv}(\texttt{embedPSet}(x),
\texttt{embedPSet}(y)) \Rightarrow \texttt{PSet.Equiv}\,x\,y$.
Proved by structural induction on \texttt{PSet} (uses well-foundedness).

$\texttt{embedOSet} := \texttt{Quotient.lift}(\texttt{OSetZFA.mk} \circ
\texttt{embedPSet}) : \mathrm{OSet} \to \mathrm{OSetZFA}$ (\texttt{noncomputable}).
$\texttt{embedOSet\_mk}(p) : \texttt{embedOSet}(\llbracket p \rrbracket) =
\llbracket\texttt{embedPSet}(p)\rrbracket$ by \texttt{rfl}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Injectivity and membership preservation]
\label{thm:embedOSet_props}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.embedOSet_injective, VR.SetsZFA.embedOSet_mem}
\leanok
\uses{def:embedOSet, def:OSetZFA_mem}
$\texttt{embedOSet\_injective}$: $\texttt{Function.Injective}(\texttt{embedOSet})$.
Proof: $\texttt{embedOSet}(x) = \texttt{embedOSet}(y) \Rightarrow
\texttt{embedPSet}(x') \approx \texttt{embedPSet}(y')$ (by \texttt{OSetZFA.exact})
$\Rightarrow \texttt{PSet.Equiv}\,x'\,y'$ (by \texttt{embedPSet\_faithful})
$\Rightarrow x = y$ (by quotient injectivity).

$\texttt{embedOSet\_mem}$: $\texttt{embedOSet}(x) \in \texttt{embedOSet}(a)
\leftrightarrow x \in a$ (membership preserved in both directions).
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[Quine atom and omega chain]
\label{def:quineAtom}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom, VR.SetsZFA.omegaChain}
\leanok
\uses{def:graphDecoration}
$\texttt{quineAtom} := \texttt{graphDecoration}(\lambda\,\_\,\_,\; \top)(())
: \mathrm{OSetZFA}.{0}$
--- decoration of the one-vertex self-loop $(V = \texttt{Unit},\;
E\,\_\,\_ = \top)$.

$\texttt{omegaChain} := \texttt{graphDecoration}((n, m) \mapsto m = n+1)
: \mathbb{N} \to \mathrm{OSetZFA}.{0}$
--- decoration of the forward-chain graph.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Quine atom self-membership]
\label{thm:quineAtom_self_mem}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom_mem_iff, VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom_self_mem}
\leanok
\uses{def:quineAtom, def:graphDecoration, thm:AFA_in_OSetZFA}
$\texttt{quineAtom\_mem\_iff}(z)$: $z \in \texttt{quineAtom}
\leftrightarrow z = \texttt{quineAtom}$ (the atom contains exactly itself).
Proof via \texttt{graphDecoration\_isDecoration} at $() : \texttt{Unit}$:
existential over \texttt{Unit} forces $w = ()$.

$\texttt{quineAtom\_self\_mem} : \texttt{quineAtom} \in \texttt{quineAtom}$.
Proof: \texttt{quineAtom\_mem\_iff quineAtom} and \texttt{rfl}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Bisimulation collapse: two-cycle = Quine atom]
\label{thm:cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.cycleDecoration, VR.SetsZFA.cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}
\leanok
\uses{def:graphDecoration, def:CoPSet_equiv}
$\texttt{cycleDecoration} := \texttt{graphDecoration}(x, y \mapsto x \ne y)
: \texttt{Bool} \to \mathrm{OSetZFA}.{0}$
--- decoration of the two-vertex mutual-edge graph.

$\texttt{cycleDecoration\_eq\_quineAtom}(b)$: both nodes of the two-cycle
equal the Quine atom.  Proof by bisimulation:
$R := \{(\texttt{cycleA}, \texttt{quineA}), (\texttt{cycleB}, \texttt{quineA})\}$
where $\texttt{cycleA/B}$ are the two CoPSet nodes and $\texttt{quineA}$
is the Quine atom's CoPSet representative.  $R$ is a bisimulation because
each cycle-node has exactly one child (the other node) and the Quine-node
has exactly one child (itself); \texttt{CoPSet.bisim\_imp\_Equiv} closes.

This is the \emph{bisimulation collapse} theorem: different-looking APGs
produce the same \texttt{OSetZFA} element when cobisimilar.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[OSetZFA membership is not well-founded]
\label{thm:OSetZFA_mem_not_wf}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA_mem_not_wf}
\leanok
\uses{thm:quineAtom_self_mem}
$\lnot\,\texttt{WellFounded}({\cdot} \in {\cdot} : \mathrm{OSetZFA} \to
\mathrm{OSetZFA} \to \mathrm{Prop})$.
Proof: accessibility implies irreflexivity (\texttt{acc\_irrefl}); if
membership were well-founded, $\texttt{quineAtom}$ would be accessible,
hence $\texttt{quineAtom} \notin \texttt{quineAtom}$; contradicts
\texttt{quineAtom\_self\_mem}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Quine atom not in range of embedding]
\label{thm:quineAtom_not_in_range}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom_not_in_range_embedOSet}
\leanok
\uses{thm:quineAtom_self_mem, thm:embedOSet_props}
$\texttt{quineAtom} \notin \texttt{Set.range}(\texttt{embedOSet})$.
Proof: suppose $\texttt{embedOSet}(x) = \texttt{quineAtom}$; then
$x \in x$ (from $\texttt{quineAtom\_self\_mem}$ and \texttt{embedOSet\_mem});
but $\texttt{ZFSet.mem\_wf}$ gives $x$ is accessible, hence $x \notin x$
by \texttt{acc\_irrefl}.  Contradiction.
This witnesses that $(\mathrm{OSet}, \in) \subsetneq (\mathrm{OSetZFA}, \in)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Omega chain descent]
\label{thm:omegaChain}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.omegaChain_mem_iff, VR.SetsZFA.omegaChain_descent}
\leanok
\uses{def:quineAtom, thm:AFA_in_OSetZFA}
$\texttt{omegaChain\_mem\_iff}(n, z)$: $z \in \texttt{omegaChain}(n)
\leftrightarrow z = \texttt{omegaChain}(n+1)$ (each element contains
exactly the next).
$\texttt{omegaChain\_descent}(n)$: $\texttt{omegaChain}(n+1) \in
\texttt{omegaChain}(n)$.

The omega chain is an infinite $\in$-descending sequence without cycles:
no element contains itself.  This is a second, distinct mode of
non-well-foundedness from \texttt{quineAtom} (self-membership
vs.\ infinite descent without cycles).
\end{theorem}

\section{Methodological observations}
\label{sec:setszfa_method}

\begin{remark}[M1 --- Coinductive vs inductive: dual construction]
\label{rem:M1_dual}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.CoPSet}
\leanok
\uses{def:CoPSetFunctor}
\texttt{PSet} (inductive, mathlib) and \texttt{CoPSet} (greatest fixpoint
of the same functor) are structurally dual: both have shape $(\alpha :
\mathrm{Type}\,u,\; A : \alpha \to \cdot)$.  The difference is generation
principle: least fixpoint (PSet, well-founded by construction) vs.\ greatest
fixpoint (CoPSet, admits cycles and infinite depth).
The two-chapter arc (Ch.~\ref{chap:sets} refutes AFA in PSet;
Ch.~\ref{chap:setszfa} proves AFA in OSetZFA) is the formal expression of
this duality.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M2 --- Final coalgebra gives AFA for free]
\label{rem:M2_final_coalg}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.AFA_in_OSetZFA}
\leanok
\uses{thm:AFA_in_OSetZFA}
\texttt{CoPSet.corec} (existence) and \texttt{CoPSet.bisim\_imp\_Equiv}
(uniqueness) together give AFA without any additional axiom.  The key
insight: a directed graph $(V, E)$ determines a coalgebra
$\texttt{graphCoalg}(E) : V \to \texttt{CoPSetFunctor}\,V$; the final
coalgebra morphism is the decoration.  This is Aczel's AFA theorem
reformulated as a property of the M-type, not postulated.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M3 --- Quotient is necessary: CoPSet $\ne$ OSetZFA]
\label{rem:M3_quotient}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.OSetZFA}
\leanok
\uses{def:OSetZFA, thm:cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}
$\texttt{OSetZFA} = \texttt{Quotient CoPSet.instSetoid}$ genuinely collapses
non-equal CoPSets.  Example: the self-loop CoPSet and one component of
the two-cycle CoPSet are distinct as M-type elements but become the same
element in OSetZFA (Theorem~\ref{thm:cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}).
The quotient is not a formality: different-shaped CoPSets can represent
the same ZFA set.  This parallels \texttt{OSet = ZFSet = Quotient PSet.setoid}
where different PSet shapes collapse under bisimulation.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M4 --- Bisimulation collapse: APG identity]
\label{rem:M4_bisim_collapse}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}
\leanok
\uses{thm:cycleDecoration_eq_quineAtom}
The self-loop APG (one vertex, self-edge) and the two-cycle APG (two
vertices, mutual edges) are cobisimilar; their decorations in OSetZFA
are equal.  This is the correct behaviour of the extensional membership
quotient: a ZFA set is determined by its membership graph up to
bisimulation, not by syntactic representation.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M5 --- Embedding asymmetry: forward vs backward]
\label{rem:M5_embed_asymm}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet_congr, VR.SetsZFA.embedPSet_faithful}
\leanok
\uses{def:embedOSet}
Forward (\texttt{embedPSet\_congr}: PSet.Equiv $\Rightarrow$ CoPSet.Equiv):
proved by coinductive bisimulation, no induction.  Providing one
bisimulation suffices.  Backward (\texttt{embedPSet\_faithful}:
CoPSet.Equiv $\Rightarrow$ PSet.Equiv): requires structural induction on
PSet.  PSet's well-foundedness is used as a proof resource to descend
through the coinductive equivalence.  The asymmetry is structural:
the forward direction is a coinductive free lunch; the backward direction
pays the cost of well-foundedness.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M6 --- ZFC $\subsetneq$ ZFA witnessed concretely]
\label{rem:M6_strict_inclusion}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom_not_in_range_embedOSet}
\leanok
\uses{thm:quineAtom_not_in_range}
$\texttt{embedOSet}$ is injective (\texttt{embedOSet\_injective}) but not
surjective (\texttt{quineAtom\_not\_in\_range\_embedOSet}).  The strict
inclusion $(\mathrm{OSet}, \in) \subsetneq (\mathrm{OSetZFA}, \in)$ is
not merely asserted but machine-verified: the Quine atom is the explicit
witness of surjectivity failure.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[M7 --- Two modes of non-well-foundedness]
\label{rem:M7_two_modes}
\lean{VR.SetsZFA.quineAtom_self_mem, VR.SetsZFA.omegaChain_descent}
\leanok
\uses{thm:quineAtom_self_mem, thm:omegaChain}
VR-Sets-ZFA provides two distinct witnesses of non-well-foundedness:
(1)~\texttt{quineAtom}: a cyclic set ($q \in q$, self-membership);
(2)~\texttt{omegaChain}: an acyclic infinite descent
($\texttt{omegaChain}(n+1) \in \texttt{omegaChain}(n)$ for all $n$,
no element is self-membered).  Both are machine-verified.
\end{remark}

\section{Position relative to existing work (Part VII)}
\label{sec:setszfa_comparison}

\begin{remark}[Comparison with Aczel (1988)]
\label{rem:compare_aczel}
\leanok
Aczel's AFA (1988) is a classical axiom added to ZF.  VR-Sets-ZFA proves
AFA as a theorem of type theory via the M-type final coalgebra.  The axiom
content is absorbed into the inductive/coinductive infrastructure of Lean~4.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Comparison with Paulson's Isabelle/ZF]
\label{rem:compare_paulson}
\leanok
Paulson's Isabelle/ZF formalises classical ZF via first-order axioms.
AFA is not part of the standard Isabelle/ZF library.  VR-Sets-ZFA provides
AFA as a theorem in a type-theoretic framework without axiom extension.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Comparison with Gylterud (HoTT)]
\label{rem:compare_gylterud}
\leanok
Gylterud (2018) constructs sets in Homotopy Type Theory using HITs.
VR-Sets-ZFA uses an M-type (greatest fixpoint) in plain dependent type
theory (no univalence, no HITs).  Both obtain AFA as a theorem; the
route and the ambient type theory differ.
\end{remark}
