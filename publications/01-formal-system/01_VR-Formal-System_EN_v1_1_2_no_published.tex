\noindent\textbf{Scope.} This is the revision delta for \emph{VR. A Formal System} v1.1.2. \emph{No
construction, definition, theorem, or axiom-profile is altered.} The single change is an added
clarification --- an interpretive-layer paragraph --- stating the ontological status of the nullary
base \emph{positively}. v1.1.1 already says, correctly, that ``$\emptyset$ is a nullary operation, not a
posited thing; there are only operations''. That is the negative half (no ontology of substance); this
note adds the positive half, so the reading is not mistaken for a claim of \emph{zero} ontology.

\section*{The clarification (operational reading --- the status of the base)}

Stating only that $\emptyset$ is ``not a posited thing'' leaves open the natural objection: to posit that
there \emph{is} an operation is itself a commitment. The corrected reading owns this. Add, as an
operational-reading remark:

\begin{quote}
The claim ``there are only operations; $\emptyset$ is a nullary operation, not a posited thing'' removes
any ontology \emph{of substance} --- no object that \emph{is} prior to operations. It does not claim the
absence of ontology altogether. To posit that there is an operation --- that there is doing --- is itself
a minimal ontological commitment, and VR makes exactly one: this single act. The commitment is not
arbitrary but \emph{self-affirming}: to deny that there is operation is already to perform one (the
denial). VR's base is therefore best described not as ``no ontology'' but as the \emph{thinnest,
self-affirming} ontology --- one act, ``there is doing'', nothing further presupposed.
\end{quote}

\noindent A fuller transcendental treatment (the retortion structure; and why grounding the operation in
a \emph{physical} fact does not help --- it only trades a thin commitment for the heavier one of
physicalism, and makes necessary arithmetic hostage to contingent physics) is deferred to the
accompanying philosophical essay, not carried in this formal work.

\section*{Why VR. A Formal System is otherwise unaffected}

The change is interpretive only. The formal content --- the free term-algebra on
$\{\emptyset\ (\text{nullary}),\ t\ (\text{unary})\}$ with the recursor, membership defined, A3
(succession) and A4 (induction) as theorems, and the axiom-free profile $[\,]$ --- is untouched, as is
the Lean module \texttt{VRCycle.VR}. The nullary base $\emptyset$ is characterised exactly as before by
$\forall x\,\neg(x \in \emptyset)$; only its \emph{reading} is made precise.
This note refers to \emph{VR. A Formal System} v1.1.1 (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20688164; concept
10.5281/zenodo.20212091).
