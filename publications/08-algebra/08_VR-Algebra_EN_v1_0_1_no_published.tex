\noindent\textbf{Scope.} This is the revision delta for VR-Algebra v1.0.1. \emph{No structure,
definition, theorem, finding, or axiom-profile of VR-Algebra is altered.} The single change is
editorial: the recall of the parent work VR (and its citation) is brought up to date with VR v1.1.1.

\section*{The correction}

The preliminaries described VR as

\begin{quote}
``VR. A Formal System (Reznik, 2026; Zenodo DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.20212092}) --- a minimal
axiomatisation of arithmetic on \textbf{three primitives} $\{\emptyset,\to,t\}$ and \textbf{four
axioms}, equivalent to Peano arithmetic.''
\end{quote}

This is superseded on two counts. (i) \textbf{The primitive count and the axioms.} In VR v1.1.1 the
propositional implication $\to$ (and the principles A1, A2 and the two-valued logical register) are
\textbf{removed}: $\to$ generated only a finite truth-function algebra used by no VR theorem ---
arithmetic, not logic --- and VR generates no logic of its own. (ii) \textbf{The DOI} cited
(\texttt{20212092}) is the original v1.0; the current version is v1.1.1. The corrected recall reads

\begin{quote}
``VR. A Formal System (Reznik, 2026; Zenodo DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.20688164}, v1.1.1; concept
\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.20212091}) --- a minimal \emph{axiom-free} reconstruction of arithmetic on
\textbf{two primitives} $\{\emptyset, t\}$, with no axioms of its own, equivalent to first-order
Peano arithmetic.''
\end{quote}

\section*{Why VR-Algebra is otherwise unaffected}

VR-Algebra (the operational tracking discipline applied to algebraic structures) does not depend on
the removed layer: it references VR only as the foundational arithmetic, through the operational
reading and the nullary base $\emptyset$, unchanged in v1.1.1. The Lean module
(\texttt{VRCycle.Algebra}) contains no reference to the removed objects (\texttt{VRBool},
\texttt{impl}, A1, A2), and the build and axiom profiles are unaffected.
