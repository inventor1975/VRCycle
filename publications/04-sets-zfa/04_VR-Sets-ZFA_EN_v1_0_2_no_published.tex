\section{(A) Relation to VR-Sets (Brouwer Edition): the cost of the quotient}

VR-Sets-ZFA was motivated (\S I.2) by the observation that the inductive \texttt{PSet} foundation of
VR-Sets makes non-well-foundedness impossible in \texttt{OSet} (\texttt{AFA\_Refuted},
\texttt{quineAtom\_impossible}), so a \emph{coinductive} universe \OSetZFA{} was built to obtain AFA.
That motivation is now \textbf{historical}. VR-Sets has since been rebuilt on the Brouwer path
(VR-Sets, Brouwer Edition) on a different carrier --- the pointed graph \OpSet, with identity carried
as a \emph{witnessed bisimulation and no quotient}. There the Quine atom is a one-vertex self-loop and
Anti-Foundation is a theorem with axiom profile \texttt{[]} (fully axiom-free,
\texttt{OpSet.afa}): a member is the same graph re-pointed, so nesting --- including self-membership
--- is native, and decoration is almost definitional.

VR-Sets-ZFA's actual, current role is the \textbf{quotiented twin} of that construction, and the pair
is itself a result: the \emph{same} content (Anti-Foundation) costs \texttt{[]} in the relational
form (\OpSet) and the full ceiling \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]} in the quotiented
form (\OSetZFA). Forming the quotient Skolemises the bisimulation's existential witnesses --- VR's
transit asymmetry --- and uniqueness must select representatives by \texttt{Classical.choice} and run
\texttt{PFunctor.M.bisim} through the choice-pulling destructor \texttt{dest}. This is the sharpest
quantitative illustration of the transit asymmetry in the cycle: not ``the quotient pulls choice
somewhere'', but one theorem, its profile before and after the quotient.

\begin{rem}[The two universes, read together]
We read the two works together as the equivalence $\OSetZFA \simeq \OpSet/\!\eq$, with VR-Sets-ZFA the
quotient of which the Brouwer Edition is the choice-free floor. \textbf{This equivalence is a
prose-level reading, not yet machine-checked} (in the modality of Conjecture~IV.2); its formalisation
--- the \texttt{M.corec} unfolding of a graph, descent through the quotient, and bijectivity on the
$\eq$-classes --- is a single \texttt{Bridge} file, a candidate for v1.1.
\end{rem}

\section{(C) ZF$^-$ axioms on \OSetZFA}

v1.0.1 proved AFA on \OSetZFA{} and a strict $\in$-preserving embedding of the well-founded universe,
but it did \emph{not} verify the ZF axioms on \OSetZFA{} itself --- only $\varnothing$, singleton and
extensionality were present. They are now all machine-checked, so that \OSetZFA{} is a model of ZF
minus Foundation (Foundation being supplied by AFA).

\begin{thm}[Pairing, Union, Power]
$z \in \{a,b\} \leftrightarrow z = a \vee z = b$ (\texttt{mem\_pair});
$z \in \bigcup x \leftrightarrow \exists y,\ y \in x \wedge z \in y$ (\texttt{mem\_sUnion});
$z \in \wp\,x \leftrightarrow z \subseteq x$ (\texttt{mem\_powerset}). Each is built at the
\texttt{CoPSet} level as a single \texttt{mk}\,$\alpha\,A$ touching only the top branching levels (the
coinductive structure affects only equality, supplied by \texttt{isBisim\_Equiv} /
\texttt{bisim\_imp\_Equiv}); the workhorse \texttt{CoPSet.mk\_equiv\_of\_coverage} reduces each
well-definedness obligation to a child-coverage check. Power set is a verbatim \texttt{PSet.powerset}
calque --- no special difficulty from the coinductive quotient.
\end{thm}

\begin{thm}[Separation and Replacement --- operational]
For a predicate $p:\OSetZFA\to\mathrm{Prop}$,
$z \in \{w \in x \mid p\,w\} \leftrightarrow z \in x \wedge p\,z$ (\texttt{mem\_sep}); for a function
$F:\OSetZFA\to\OSetZFA$, $z \in \{F\,y \mid y \in x\} \leftrightarrow \exists y,\ y \in x \wedge
F\,y = z$ (\texttt{mem\_image}). Because $p$ and $F$ are first-class objects \emph{over the quotient},
they are automatically $\eq$-invariant: the collapse of the first-order Separation/Replacement
\emph{schemas} into single quantified-over-an-object statements is the operational reification of
describability, not a generic type-theoretic accident.
\end{thm}

\begin{thm}[Infinity]
With the von Neumann successor $\mathrm{succ}\,x := \texttt{insert}\,x\,x = x\cup\{x\}$ and $\omega$
the set of von Neumann naturals: $\varnothing \in \omega$ and $\forall x,\ x \in \omega \to
\mathrm{succ}\,x \in \omega$ (\texttt{infinity}); $z \in \omega \leftrightarrow \exists n,\ z =
\llbracket\texttt{ofNat}\,n\rrbracket$ (\texttt{mem\_omega}). The naturals match VR's $O_n$ and
mathlib's \texttt{ZFSet.omega}.
\end{thm}

\begin{rem}[\OSetZFA{} models ZF$^-$, at the M-type ceiling]
With Extensionality, $\varnothing$, Pairing, Union, Power, Separation, Replacement and Infinity
verified, and Foundation supplied as AFA, every member of \OSetZFA{} obeys the ZF$^-$ axioms as
machine-checked theorems. All witnesses sit at \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]} --- the
M-type ceiling inherited from \texttt{PFunctor.M.dest}, not the set theory (the choice-free
counterparts live on the \OpSet{} carrier, \S1). \textbf{Honest boundary:} each axiom holds in
\OSetZFA{} as a theorem; \OSetZFA{} is not itself given a first-order satisfaction relation --- that
``$\models$ ZF$^-$'' reading stays metatheory, as elsewhere in the cycle.
\end{rem}

\section{(B) Correction to \S IV.4 --- ZFC $\subsetneq$ ZFA earned}

v1.0.1's \S IV.4 stated ``ZFC $\subsetneq$ ZFA''. What it had machine-checked was the strict inclusion
of \emph{carriers with membership}: \texttt{embedOSet} is injective (\texttt{embedOSet\_injective}) and
$\in$-preserving (\texttt{embedOSet\_mem}), and the Quine atom witnesses non-surjectivity
(\texttt{quineAtom\_not\_in\_range\_embedOSet}). That is an inclusion of \emph{universes}, not yet of
\emph{theories}: ``ZFA universe'' was not fully earned because the ZF$^-$ axioms were not verified on
\OSetZFA. With \S2 above, the gap is closed.

\begin{thm}[\S IV.4, corrected]
$\OSetZFA{}$ is a model of ZF$^-$ (\S2); \texttt{OSet} is the grounded (well-founded) fragment, and
$(\texttt{OSet},\in)\subsetneq(\OSetZFA,\in)$ strictly, with the Quine atom the explicit witness of the
strictness. Hence the inclusion is of \emph{universes both modelling ZF$^-$}, the well-founded one
properly inside the one admitting cycles --- the earned form of ``ZFC $\subsetneq$ ZFA''.
\end{thm}

\noindent The boundary of the previous remark applies: this is an inclusion of machine-checked models,
not a metatheoretic theorem-set inclusion (which stays, as throughout the cycle, in the metatheory).
