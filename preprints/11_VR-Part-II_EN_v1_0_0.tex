\begin{abstract}
The VR cycle built an operational mathematics --- arithmetic, numbers, sets,
forms, topology, a continuum on Brouwer's path --- and only afterwards wrote
out the logic it had been standing on: ZTL, Zero-Trust Logic, a two-valued
logic over marked (unverified) inputs generated by the single principle
\emph{truth is never granted on credit} (Zenodo, concept DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21318981). This preprint carries out the programme ``raise VR
onto ZTL'' and verifies, rather than declares, the thesis that \emph{VR
always stood on ZTL}. Three steps, every claim either \MEASURED{} (machine
enumeration, reproducible by the test stands of the ZTL repository) or
kernel-checked in Lean~4 with the axiom footprint printed per object.
(a)~\emph{Witnessed identity is a ZTL atom discipline}: the operational
set universe \textsf{VRCycle.SetsOp} carries identity as a bisimulation
witness; we package verdicts \emph{with} their certificates
(\texttt{EqVerdict}, \texttt{MemVerdict}), show that the ZTL inference rules
alive on identity atoms are exactly witness constructors, that identity on
finite operational sets and on the von Neumann register is \emph{totally
earnable}, that groundedness of a set is orthogonal to earnability of its
identity (the cycle that dooms the liar is harmless in $\Omega=\{\Omega\}$),
and that a fully earned register is classical --- greedy zero-trust verdicts
over decided atoms neither lie nor refuse. The entire verdict layer sits on
the \emph{empty} axiom list. Step (a) also returned a correction to ZTL
itself: its verdict-warranty equivalence claim proved pool-relative; the
warranty is a two-grade ladder (sound --- never lies; hereditary --- never
revoked), published same-day as ZTL v1.1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21323552).
(b)~\emph{Choice sequences are the lazy register}: for a growing binary
sequence, what a stage may assert is what the prefix forces over admitted
continuations; we measure and then kernel-check that the lawless stage court
coincides with ZTL's global supervaluation \emph{totally} (a law is
knowledge: it narrows the worlds and strictly strengthens the stage), that
Kripke persistence --- refused as a free axiom by the greedy register --- is
native to the lazy one, that warranted greedy verdicts are exactly the
Brouwer-assertable ones, and that the fallen law of identity $p\to p$ is
\emph{redeemed} by the stage court: a law of logic, not of data.
(c)~\emph{The survival ledger}: a proof survives the move onto ZTL iff it
stands below the classical floor --- ZTL's fallen laws are laws of free
truth, their Lean fingerprint is \texttt{Classical.choice}, and a
constructive proof from earned premises is a chain of ZTL-alive rules. The
cycle's four-tier axiom ledger was therefore the ZTL-survival audit all
along; sweeping 405 live-audited objects plus flagship anchors shows that
everything VR calls operational moves, and what stays is exactly what the
cycle had already flagged as classical by design, by substrate, or by
borrowed plumbing. No operational theorem died in the move.
\\[3pt]
\emph{Keywords:} zero-trust logic, three-valued logic, witnessed
bisimulation, operational set theory, choice sequences, supervaluation,
Kripke persistence, intuitionism, constructive mathematics, axiom audit,
Lean~4, machine-checked proofs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Brouwer built a mathematics first; Heyting wrote out its logic afterwards.
The VR cycle repeated the pattern at its own scale: ten works of operational
mathematics --- an axiom-free reconstruction of arithmetic, operational
number superstructures, two set-theoretic registers, a two-register
apparatus of forms, formal topology, a continuum on Brouwer's path --- were
built under a uniform discipline (operations before objects, witnesses
before truths, an axiom audit per object) before the logic of that
discipline was written down. The logic, when it was finally written out and
machine-checked, is ZTL --- \emph{Zero-Trust Logic}~[1]: exactly two
truth values (verdicts are always classical); the mark \vZ{} on an
unverified input --- a symbol of the calculating tables but not a truth
value, and in the system's \emph{genetic} alphabet
$\mathsf{N},\vF,\vT,\vZ$ the fourth and last letter ($\mathsf{N}$, the
solver's phase, is born of nothing and never enters a table; \vF{} and
\vT{} are the first generated values; \vZ{} presupposes both --- its
classical readings are exactly $\{\vT,\vF\}$); and one
generating principle for all connectives --- \emph{a connective returns
\vT{} only if \vT{} is forced under every classical reading of the
unverified}. Truth is never granted on credit.

The present work is Part II of that story: the programme ``raise VR onto
ZTL,'' stated by the curator as three steps and a thesis. The thesis ---
\emph{VR always stood on ZTL} --- is attractive and dangerous in equal
measure: attractive because the two disciplines visibly rhyme (witnessed
identity looks like earned truth; apartness earned at a finite stage looks
like a verdict with a certificate), dangerous because rhymes are cheap. The
VR cycle's standing rule is that such claims are \emph{verified, not
postulated}. Accordingly, everything below carries one of two tags:
\MEASURED{} --- established by exhaustive machine enumeration, reproducible
by the ZTL repository's regression stands (E20--E22) --- or a Lean~4
theorem, with \texttt{\#print axioms} output quoted per object.

The three steps, and one find that travelled in the opposite direction:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Step (a)} (\S\ref{sec:atoms}): the witnessed identity of the
operational set universe \emph{is} a ZTL atom discipline. Verdicts are
packaged with their certificates; soundness is the shape of the type, not a
theorem about it; the alive inference rules are witness constructors; finite
and von Neumann identity is totally earnable; a fully earned register is
classical. The whole layer sits on the \emph{empty} axiom list.
\item \textbf{The return find} (\S\ref{sec:ladder}): the identity atoms
falsified a claim of ZTL v1.0 --- the warranty of a verdict is not one bit
but a two-grade ladder. The correction was published the same day as ZTL
v1.1. Part II thus begins by demonstrating the direction of authority: the
measured corpus corrects the logic, not the other way round.
\item \textbf{Step (b)} (\S\ref{sec:stages}): Brouwer's two objects --- the
sequence-in-growth and the assertion-at-a-stage --- are ZTL's two registers.
The lawless stage court coincides with global supervaluation totally;
persistence is native to the lazy register; $p \to p$ is redeemed at the
stage.
\item \textbf{Step (c)} (\S\ref{sec:survival}): the survival criterion and
the ledger. The cycle's own four-tier axiom audit turns out to be the
ZTL-survival audit; the operational corpus moves wholesale.
\end{itemize}

Before the steps, \S\ref{sec:delta} discharges a debt any such programme
owes: ZTL's slogan could be Brouwer's, so one must show the logic is not
intuitionism rediscovered. The answer is measured: the two are
\emph{incomparable} as law-sets, agree exactly on a battery of premised
rules, and part ways on every structural signature that defines
intuitionism.

\paragraph{Code and reproducibility.} The ZTL side lives in the ZTL
repository (test stands \texttt{zipc.py}, \texttt{zopsets.py},
\texttt{zchoice.py} --- expeditions E20--E22 --- inside a 30-stand
regression, plus ten Lean modules on the empty axiom list). The VR side
lives in the VRCycle repository: the Lean module \textsf{VRCycle.SetsZTL}
(\texttt{Kernel}, \texttt{Atoms}, \texttt{Stages}, \texttt{Survival}), the
survival ledger \texttt{ZTL\_SURVIVAL.md}, and blueprint chapter~14. The
ZTL value kernel is \emph{vendored} into VRCycle with a commit pin; the
dependency is one-way --- ZTL never depends on VR.

\section{The two sides of the stitch}
\label{sec:sides}

\paragraph{ZTL in one paragraph.} Values \vT{}, \vF{}; input mark \vZ{}. For
a classical connective $f$, its ZTL lift returns \vT{} iff $f$ returns true
under \emph{every} classical reading of the \vZ-arguments (each occurrence
read independently); otherwise \vF{}. Compound formulas are always
classical: \vZ{} lives only on atoms. The measured price list of v1.0: 12
classical laws survive, 14 fall, and every fallen law is a law of ``free
truth'' --- it mints a verdict for an unverified atom ($p\to p$, $q \to
(p \to q)$, excluded middle, double-negation elimination, both De Morgan
laws as identities, idempotence\dots). The discovered split: of 14 classical
\emph{premised rules}, 12 survive --- inference transports earned truth;
laws mint it. Verification is an act ($\vZ \to \vT/\vF$), and a verdict is a
pair (value, warranty); after the Part-II correction (\S\ref{sec:ladder})
the warranty is a two-grade ladder. Everything is machine-checked in Lean~4
with an \emph{empty} axiom list~[1].

\paragraph{VR in one paragraph.} The operational register of VR's set
theory, \textsf{VRCycle.SetsOp}~[2], carries a set as a
\emph{revealing functionality} (a pointed graph) and identity as a
\emph{witnessed bisimulation} --- a relation carried with its witness, never
collapsed to a quotient (the collapse is what pulls choice). Foundation is a
predicate, not an axiom: the ZFC-like and ZFA-like universes are fragments
of one domain. The continuum~[4] is built on Brouwer's path:
branches of the binary spread with their finite performed segments. The
cycle's uniform discipline is the axiom audit: every public object prints
its axioms, and the corpus is stratified into four tiers, from the empty
list to the classical tier with \texttt{Classical.choice}.

\paragraph{The stitch, in one sentence.} An identity atom $x \eq y$ of the
operational universe \emph{is} a ZTL atom: earned \vT{} carries the
bisimulation, earned \vF{} carries the refutation (apartness at a finite
separation stage), and the unexamined atom is worth exactly the input mark
\vZ{}.

\section{Not intuitionism rediscovered (\MEASURED)}
\label{sec:delta}

ZTL's slogan --- no truth on credit --- could be read as Brouwer's, and VR's
continuum walks Brouwer's road; so Part II owes an explicit delta. It is
measured by expedition E20: a calibrated decision procedure for
intuitionistic propositional logic (Dyckhoff's contraction-free
G4ip~[8]; ``IPC refuses'' below means \emph{non-theorem}, not
``search failed'') against ZTL's native entailment, on a 27-law canonical
battery and the 14-rule premised battery.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Law-sets are incomparable} inside classical logic, both
directions witnessed. IPC-valid but ZTL-refused: 6 witnesses, including
$p \to p$ and $q \to (p\to q)$. ZTL-valid but IPC-refused: the weak law of
excluded middle $\lnot p \lor \lnot\lnot p$ --- Jankov's axiom, the axiom of
the intermediate logic KC~[9]. Since every intermediate logic
proves $p\to p$, ZTL is \emph{not} an intermediate logic: it sits beside
IPC, not above it.
\item \textbf{Premised rules coincide 14/14}, including both casualties
($\lnot\lnot$-elimination; tautology-in-conclusion). The diagnoses differ
--- no construction of $p$ versus a laundered mark --- but the casualty list
is identical: both systems refuse inference moves that mint a certificate
out of nothing. (As full consequence relations they differ already at empty
premises: $p \to p$.)
\item \textbf{Every structural signature separates.} ZTL is a finite matrix;
IPC provably has none (G\"odel 1932~[6]). IPC has the disjunction
property; ZTL loses it (measured witness and census: its $\lor$ is
supervaluational, not BHK). ZTL's double negation is verdict-transparent
($\models A \iff \models \lnot\lnot A$, checked totally on a 786-formula
pool); IPC's is load-bearing (Glivenko~[7], verified 27/27 by
the prover as a workout). An unproved sentence in intuitionism is an open
potentiality; a ZTL refusal is a terminating answer with a passport.
\end{itemize}

Same slogan, different creditors: intuitionism refuses truth without a
\emph{construction}; ZTL refuses it without \emph{certified input}.
Proof-debt versus data-debt. This is the licence for Part II: raising VR
onto ZTL is a move onto a third foundation posture, not a relabelling of
the second.

\section{Step (a): witnessed identity as earned truth}
\label{sec:atoms}

\subsection{The measured half (E21)}

Finite operational sets are modelled exactly as \OpSet{} builds them ---
pointed reveal-graphs --- and identity atoms are judged by a bisimulation
earner. \MEASURED{} on a zoo including the von Neumann naturals, duplicated
representations, and the Quine atom $\Omega = \{\Omega\}$:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Identity is totally earnable}: every atom decides --- \vT{}
with a checkable witness (the relation is re-verified independently of the
search), \vF{} with a finite separation stage, in the apartness discipline
of ZTL's stream expedition. On finite carriers \vZ{} is an input state, not
a fate. (Contrast streams, \S\ref{sec:stages}: there equality is
\vZ-permanent --- finiteness is what buys totality, not the logic.)
\item \textbf{Deduplication is earned}: $\{\emptyset,\emptyset\} \eq
\{\emptyset\}$ with an explicit witness. ZTL's set expedition had measured
$\{\vZ,\vZ\} \neq \{\vZ\}$ --- merging \emph{unverified} members is not
earned; together the two halves close one story: deduplication happens
exactly when identity is witnessed.
\item \textbf{Alive rules are witness constructors}: reflexivity is the
diagonal, symmetry the converse, transitivity the composition, congruence
the union --- 44 constructed witnesses on the zoo, every one re-checked.
The ZTL rules alive on $\eq$-atoms are not table luck.
\item \textbf{Cardinality is an interval that verification narrows
monotonically}: $|\{x,y,z\}| \in [1,3] \supseteq [1,2] \supseteq [2,2]$
along truthful verification of the identity atoms, with the last atom earned
\emph{free} by partition consistency --- witness composition working by
itself.
\item \textbf{Groundedness $\perp$ earnability}: $\Omega \eq \Omega_2$ (the
two-node cycle) is earned \vT{} while \texttt{IsGrounded}$(\Omega)$ is
false. The same cycle that dooms a sentence (the liar: permanent quarantine,
a least-fixed-point leftover) is harmless in a set (identity is
greatest-fixed-point-shaped). Anti-Foundation-as-theorem is the set-side
mirror of quarantine-as-theorem: two registers, one architecture.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The return find: the warranty is a ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}

The identity atoms then did something unplanned: they falsified a claim of
ZTL v1.0. That version asserted an equivalence --- ``a verdict is stable
under the global supervaluation iff it is invariant under every sequence of
verifications'' --- measured totally on its ten-formula pool, with a
monotonicity corollary (``a stable verdict is never revoked''). On the
3303-formula pool of E21, 96 supervaluation-stable \vT-verdicts \emph{die}
under truthful verification. The separating shape is
\textbf{or(ladder, gap)}:
\[
\lnot\lnot p \;\lor\; (q \lor \lnot q)
\]
--- greedy \vT{} via the $\lnot\lnot$ ladder, insured by a gap that is true
in \emph{all} completions yet greedy-\vF{}; verifying $p:=\vF$ kicks the
ladder away before the gap closes. A simpler cell: $(\lnot p) \to (q \to
q)$, where the gap is the fallen law of identity itself.

\begin{finding}[The two-grade warranty]
Sound (supervaluational: every completion agrees with the verdict) buys
\emph{never lies} --- measured: of 9192 sound-warranted \vT-verdicts, none
is false of the facts. Hereditary (invariant under every partial
refinement) buys \emph{never spoils} --- measured: 0 deaths, totally.
Hereditary implies sound; the converse is false (the grades separate).
\end{finding}

The correction was cross-checked with the original section's own
instruments, adopted into the ZTL code the same day (the studio now speaks
in grades: ``hereditary \vT{} --- build on it''; ``sound \vT{} --- never a
lie, may stall''), and published as ZTL v1.1~[1]. Part II's first
contribution is thus a correction flowing \emph{from} the mathematics
\emph{into} the logic --- the direction of authority the whole cycle is
built on.

\subsection{The kernel-checked half (\textsf{SetsZTL.Atoms})}

The Lean module packages a verdict \emph{with} its certificate:

\begin{defn}[\texttt{EqVerdict}]
For operational sets $x, y$: \texttt{earnedT} holds a bisimulation witness
$x \eq y$; \texttt{earnedF} holds a refutation; \texttt{unexamined} is the
zero-trust start. \texttt{val} sends the three to \vT, \vF, \vZ. Soundness
is not a theorem about the type but its shape: \texttt{sound\_T} and
\texttt{sound\_F} \emph{extract} the certificate from the value.
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[constructors; vn register; orthogonality]
\texttt{refl}, \texttt{symm} (verdict-preserving), and \texttt{composeT}
(composition of carried witnesses) are certificate constructors for the
alive rules. On the operational naturals the identity verdict
\texttt{vnVerdict n m} is never \vZ{} and equals \vT{} exactly when $n=m$.
The Quine atom earns its identity, and its apartness from $\emptyset$,
while ungrounded.
\end{thm}

\begin{thm}[The fully earned register is classical]
If every atom of a formula carries a decided, truthful verdict, the greedy
ZTL value of every formula over the six connectives is decided and
truthful: \vT{} exactly on the classically true formulas, \vZ{} nowhere.
Over verified identity atoms the zero-trust connectives neither lie nor
refuse.
\end{thm}

\paragraph{Axiom profile (printed per object).} The entire verdict layer ---
including the classicality theorem --- reports \emph{``does not depend on
any axioms''}: no choice, no quotients, not even propositional
extensionality. The vn-register theorems initially inherited
\texttt{propext} from the host module's proof style (\texttt{rw} by
\emph{iff}-lemmas); a tier pass replaced those rewrites by explicit
\emph{iff}-combinators, after which \emph{all 39 audited objects of
\textsf{SetsOp} --- including \texttt{vn\_inj} and the non-enumerability of
the operational universe --- and all of \textsf{SetsZTL} sit on the empty
axiom list}. The only remaining non-empty profile in the wing is the
describable-register encoding (borrowed pairing plumbing from the mathlib
library --- constructively true upstream facts proved there with classical
instruments; documented, removable at a known price).

\section{Step (b): choice sequences as the lazy register}
\label{sec:stages}

Brouwer's continuum distinguishes the sequence-in-growth from what is
assertable at a stage. VR's continuum took that road; ZTL, it turns out,
had already priced it: the distinction \emph{is} the two-register split.
\MEASURED{} by expedition E22 (pool of 3303 formulas over three position
atoms; all 15 prefixes of a binary horizon; the stage court judging by
forcing over admitted continuations), then kernel-checked in
\textsf{SetsZTL.Stages} against VR's own \texttt{Branch} of the binary
spread.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The ladder of judges runs through the warranty.} The naive
inclusion ``greedy \vT{} $\Rightarrow$ stage-forced \vT'' is false: 2027 of
27905 raw greedy \vT-verdicts assert what no prefix forces (witness:
$\lnot\lnot a_0$ at the root --- the E12 cell read temporally, a verdict
about a future the data does not underwrite). The true chain has 0
violations: \emph{warranted} (sound-grade) greedy \vT{} $\Rightarrow$
stage-forced \vT{} $\Rightarrow$ true on every admitted continuation. Zero
trust speaks assertably about a growing sequence exactly when it carries
its warranty --- the ladder of \S\ref{sec:ladder} vindicated from Brouwer's
side within a week of its discovery.
\item \textbf{Lawless = global supervaluation, totally} (49545
formula-prefix pairs, 0 mismatches): for a lawless sequence the stage court
\emph{is} ZTL's global supervaluation --- zero trust is assertability
against a maximally ignorant future. \textbf{A law is knowledge}: under
``exactly one 1'' the stage court forces a disjunction at stage 0 that the
supervaluation refuses (1968 such cells) --- Brouwer's lawless/lawful
divide lands on ZTL's modal frame as: \emph{which world-set the global
$\Box$ ranges over}.
\item \textbf{Heredity is native to the lazy register}: stage verdicts are
never revoked by a revealed bit (0 revocations, against 1371 greedy
deaths). Kripke persistence --- which the greedy register refuses as a free
axiom and sells back as the warranty bit --- is a theorem of the stage
court (\texttt{forces\_mono}, kernel-checked).
\item \textbf{$p \to p$ is redeemed.} At the root every branch satisfies
the identity law while the greedy court abstains ($\texttt{zimp Z Z} =
\vF$): a law of logic, not of data --- the stage court asserts it, the data
court waits. Both cells on the empty axiom list
(\texttt{identity\_forced\_at\_root}, \texttt{identity\_greedy\_abstains}).
\item \textbf{Streams reproduce the constructive asymmetry}: equality of
branches is never forced at a finite stage (two explicit continuations
diverge below any node --- \texttt{eq\_never\_forced}), apartness is earned
by one disagreeing reveal and, being an existential fact, persists
(\texttt{apart\_earned}). Measured coupling: $\mathrm{eq}=\vF \iff
\mathrm{apart}=\vT$, yet apartness is \emph{not} the ZTL-negation of
equality even at the level of marks ($\lnot\vZ=\vF\neq\vZ$; 227 cells) ---
the constructive primitivity of $\#$ falls out of the zero-trust tables.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Axiom profile.} The kernel cells are on the empty list; the
\texttt{Branch}-layer theorems (\texttt{stage\_eq\_super},
\texttt{forces\_mono}, the stream pair) inherit
$[\texttt{propext}(,\texttt{Quot.sound})]$ from the mathlib list lemmas ---
the Continuum wing's own choice-free tier, with \texttt{Classical.choice}
nowhere (one \texttt{simpa} was evicted for pulling it). Measured
lemma-by-lemma: the residue is upstream proof style, not logic; removable
at a documented price.

\section{Step (c): the survival ledger}
\label{sec:survival}

What does it mean for an operational theorem to \emph{survive} the move
onto ZTL? A naive answer --- audit the proofs for uses of the fallen laws
--- is a category error: Lean proofs live in Lean's calculus and never
manipulate \vZ{}. The criterion adopted (the curator's decision, after the
alternatives were weighed) is:

\begin{defn}[Survival criterion C2]
A proof survives the move onto ZTL iff it stands below the classical
floor: no \texttt{Classical.choice} in its measured axiom footprint.
\end{defn}

The bridge that justifies it, each link measured: ZTL's fallen laws are
exactly the laws of \emph{free truth}, and their Lean fingerprint is
classical case analysis --- applying excluded middle to a proposition is
declaring every atom pre-verified. At the level of premised rules ZTL keeps
what constructive inference keeps (\S\ref{sec:delta}: 14/14); alive rules
are witness constructors (\S\ref{sec:atoms}); so a constructive proof from
earned premises \emph{is} a chain of ZTL-alive rules, delivering the
conclusion's certificate. VR's operational theorems are premised by
construction --- operationality is earned inputs --- so they are rules, not
laws: the surviving category.

\begin{cor}[The thesis, made literal]
The four-tier axiom ledger the VR cycle has kept since its first work is
the ZTL-survival audit. ``VR always stood on ZTL'' is, under C2, a
bookkeeping theorem rather than a slogan.
\end{cor}

\paragraph{The ledger (\MEASURED).} 405 public objects live-audited by
re-elaborating the whole project, plus flagship probes of the wings whose
audits live in external ledgers. Moves wholesale: the VR formal system (the
flagship VR$\,\leftrightarrow\,$PA equivalence is \emph{axiom-free}),
VR-Forms (45/45 clean), the operational set universe (39/41; the two are
the describable-register plumbing), Part II itself (38/38), the operational
continuum (88/92), formal topology (its complete ledger reports zero
choice; the binary Tychonoff re-probed clean), the integer line, and ---
notably --- the ZF-closure facts of the \emph{classical} register, which
are themselves choice-free over their quotient carrier. Stays on the
classical shore, exactly as the cycle had already flagged: VR-Audit (0/17
--- classical by design: choice is its subject matter), VR-Sets-ZFA (3/14
--- the coinductive substrate that motivated \OpSet{} in the first place),
the $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{C}$ correspondence theorems (substrate
leak of the host library's carriers; the choice-free number line already
lives in the Continuum), the Sperner--Brouwer fixed point (the real-number
substrate), and assorted mathlib-carrier instances in Algebra/Apparatus.
\emph{No operational theorem died in the move; the fallen laws of ZTL were
never load-bearing in the operational corpus.}

\paragraph{Spot-checks (\textsf{SetsZTL.Survival}, all on the empty axiom
list).} Three flagships exhibited in the moved form. \emph{Strong
extensionality}: a law in the classical register, a \emph{rule} in the
operational one --- \texttt{extVerdict} constructs the identity certificate
from the member-matching data. \emph{Anti-Foundation}: \texttt{afaVerdict}
--- two decorations of one graph carry earned identity at every vertex.
\emph{Membership}: \texttt{MemVerdict}, the membership twin of
\texttt{EqVerdict} --- the vn membership register is total and classical,
and earned $\in$ transports along earned $\eq$ (\texttt{memCongrVerdict},
the rule form of congruence). The continuum spot-check, apartness earned at
a stage, is \S\ref{sec:stages}.

\paragraph{The reading discipline.} The audit bounds the \emph{given}
proof, never the theorem: a classical tier means \emph{this proof does not
move}, not \emph{the theorem is false on ZTL} --- the witness method is an
auditor, not a separator. And the audit is blind to impredicativity (the
cycle's power-set finding); ZTL says nothing about impredicativity either
--- a shared boundary, recorded.

\section{What Part II does not claim}
\label{sec:caveats}

\begin{itemize}
\item The rule-agreement with IPC and the law-incomparability witnesses are
battery-relative in their counts (the incomparability itself is
existential, hence robust).
\item The warranty ladder's Lean twin is deliberately \emph{not} formalised
here: it belongs to the ZTL corpus, where it is measured; VR consumes it.
\item The stage court's equivalence with supervaluation is kernel-checked
for horizon-read properties over the binary spread; richer laws enter only
as world-set restrictions, as in the measured example.
\item Criterion C2 audits proofs, not theorems (above); a semantic
criterion --- a ZTL-valued model for VR statements, truth in a model
instead of proof audit --- is stronger, was weighed, and is deferred as a
separate work.
\item Wings whose audits live in external ledgers are anchored by flagship
probes, not by a full live sweep.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

Part I of the VR story built an operational mathematics and paid for every
theorem in the currency of an axiom audit. Part II shows the currency was a
logic. Witnessed identity is earned truth (and packaging verdicts with
their certificates costs \emph{zero} axioms); Brouwer's growth/stage
distinction is the lazy/greedy register split (and the lawless stage court
is, totally, the global supervaluation); the corpus's four-tier ledger is
the survival audit, under which everything operational moves and everything
flagged classical stays, for the reasons already on record. Along the way
the mathematics corrected the logic once --- the warranty ladder, published
same-day as ZTL v1.1 --- and the logic returned the favour by explaining
the corpus's oldest discipline to itself.

Brouwer built a mathematics, and Heyting wrote out its logic. VR was built
first, and ZTL is its logic written out --- and the delta measured in
\S\ref{sec:delta} says the second pair is not a photocopy of the first.
